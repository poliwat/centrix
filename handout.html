On the questions that decide whether an American family can build a good life, things have gotten worse under both parties. We keep the best wisdom of left and right and aim all of it at one goal: a productive, self-reliant, flourishing people.

The founding argument

Three declines — worsening for ~100 years, no matter who was in chargeThese measures aren't a "left problem" or a "right problem." They fell under administrations of every party. Reversing them is why Centrix exists.

1 — A roof of your own
3×→5×
Housing left families behind
Homes cost ~3× median income in 1970, ~5× by 2024 — with median households spending ~42% of income on housing.
2 — War on the middle class
90%/33%
Pay stopped tracking work
1979–2025: productivity grew ~90% but typical pay only ~33% — ~3.5× faster. Kept pace would mean ~$16/hr more.
3 — Cost of a life
+1,200%
Essentials outran wages
Since 1980, tuition up ~1,200% and healthcare ~450% vs ~236% inflation. The dollar has lost 96%+ of its 1913 value.
There are real, honest disagreements between left and right — and we respect them. But none of these three declines belongs to one side. The fight over who wins the White House has distracted us from the fight that matters: actually empowering citizens to prosper. Our whole platform is built to reverse these curves.
The platform

What we'd do

Wages & the middle class

Centrix: Reward production directly — tax relief and low-interest capital for citizens and small firms that make and sell real goods; apprenticeships; profit-sharing so pay tracks productivity again.

Local manufacturing & the producer economy

Centrix: Open, published guides — inspired by self-reliant communities like the Amish — teaching anyone to pick a good, produce it, and sell it locally, backed by micro-grants and mentorship.

Housing & family

Centrix: Pro-family, pro-ownership tools proven across many nations — tax relief that grows per child, 0%/low-interest first-home loans, a goal of affordable homeownership, plus reform to build more homes.

Safety net & work

Centrix: Keep the net strong; phase support out gradually as income rises so it's a launch pad, not a trap. Every recipient gets the producer guides and start-up capital.

Costs & foreign policy

Centrix: Attack healthcare/tuition costs with transparency and outcome-based funding; principled non-intervention abroad, investing instead in domestic resources, energy, and technology.

Our philosophy

Keep the gold from each side

  • Producers, not dependents. Dignity comes from making things of value.
  • Localism over centralization. Prosperity built close to home is resilient.
  • A hand up, with an open door out. A real net that rewards the climb back to work.
  • Home before empire. Fund our own resources, technology, and people first.
  • Family as the foundation. Make it easier to marry, have children, own a home.
  • Freedom of conscience. Different communities may hold different values — sanctioned evenly, no side favored.
The one absolute limit: no belief, community, or movement may support, threaten, or commit violence against fellow citizens — ever. Tolerance ends where violence begins.
