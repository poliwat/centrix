7 · Localism — no clean vote yet — Honest gap. The available decentralization votes this session (CRA / REINS-type) are party-line coded — scoring them would bias the basket and fail the party-symmetry test. We use the co-sponsorship signal here, or wait for a genuinely bipartisan federalism vote, rather than force a partisan one in.

Honesty is the product. Three flags built in: reconciliation bundles many policies (omnibus judgment call); tariffs are genuinely contested within a "domestic production" philosophy (broad tariffs raise household and small-producer costs, so aligned = repeal, but targeted industrial policy is a separate question); and localism has no clean non-partisan vote this session, which we disclose rather than paper over. Every position is provisional and open to challenge — exactly as the methodology requires.
